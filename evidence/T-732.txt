T-732 - Settings is a modal dialog that keeps Tab inside it (DT-C168; UX audit lane C)
Worker 49, coordinator 57, branch s-49/t732 from main 73008fe1, 2026-11-03.
Row: proof/ux-audit/requests/W3.md, "T-732 / DT-C168", from live verify t11 (T-714 note).

WHAT WAS WRONG
- Settings (src/settings/SettingsPage.tsx) was role=dialog with no aria-modal and no Tab trap. In live verify t11,
  Shift+Tab step 15 left it and landed on the window's close button behind it. T-714's sweep (DT-C150) covered only
  elements with aria-modal="true", so it never saw Settings.

THE FIX (DT-C168)
- The Settings root gets aria-modal="true" and onKeyDown={trapTab}, the shared trap from src/chrome/dialogFocus.ts that
  the T-714 dialogs use (no new trap). Tab and Shift+Tab wrap at its ends; a Tab a dialog inside Settings already wrapped
  (default prevented) is left alone. Settings already had tabIndex -1, which trapTab needs.
- Escape is unchanged: Settings keeps its own rules (T-565); its check for a modal inside it reads only its descendants,
  so marking the root itself does not block it.
- Side effects of aria-modal, both checked:
  - src/automations/AutomationsPage.tsx counts any aria-modal as covering the page, so it leaves keys alone under
    Settings (it already did when focus was inside Settings).
  - src/onboarding/Guides.tsx tipEscape lets a modal keep Escape: while a guide tip is shown with Settings open, Escape
    now closes Settings first and the tip on the next Escape (before, one Escape closed both).
  - useWindowKeys OVERLAYS and TrustPrompt already counted any role=dialog: no change.

TESTS
- src/settings/SettingsPage.escape.test.ts "T-732: ...": mounts the real Settings, checks aria-modal, then Shift+Tab from
  its first focusable control lands on its last, and Tab from the last lands on the first. The test's fake DOM learned the
  trap's focusable list and a Shift key. Red-checked: without onKeyDown={trapTab}, 1 failed ("Shift+Tab from the first
  control stays in Settings, on its last").
- src/chrome/reach.test.ts: the T-714 table of aria-modal dialogs and their traps lists Settings (trapTab); without it 1 failed
  (27 dialogs found, 26 listed). 10 passed.
- SettingsPage.escape.test.ts: 8 passed. Full vitest: 805 files, 5999 passed and 2 failed: reach.test (the table, fixed
  above) and editor/editPlacement.test.ts "60 paragraphs" (a timing test under full-suite load; passes alone, untouched). pnpm exec tsc --noEmit -p . : clean.
