WHAT WAS WRONG
- After a 1400 to 640 px resize with the right sidebar open, the T-699 step-aside (styles.css) hid the left sidebar, but
  the top bar's left toggle kept aria-pressed=true and the title "Hide sidebar" (still stale after 1 frame, 2 frames and
  700 ms). It caught up only when something else redrew the top bar.
- Cause: shell/window/TopBar.tsx passed leftShown(sidebarOpen, isNarrow(), ...) and isNarrow() (shell/window/narrow.ts)
  read matchMedia at render with no subscription, so a resize alone never redrew the top bar.

THE FIX (DT-C141)
- narrow.ts: watchNarrow(f) sets the onchange slot of one matchMedia("(max-width: 899px)") list, the same query as the
  stylesheet and isNarrow. Each matchMedia call returns its own list, so the slot is not shared with anyone.
- shell/window/TopBar.tsx: narrow = useSyncExternalStore(watchNarrow, isNarrow), passed to leftShown in place of the
  render-time isNarrow(). React redraws the top bar when the window crosses 900 px (only then, not on every resize), and
  the toggle's aria-pressed and title follow the step-aside in the same frame.
- The subscription returns an empty unsubscribe (ponytail note in narrow.ts): the top bar lives as long as its window,
  and a late call only asks React to redraw a top bar that is gone. Clearing the slot cost the last bytes over +100 B.
- The setters (Ctrl+B, menu, palette, create rows via windowStore.ts nextLeft) already read isNarrow() live; unchanged.
- Also removed: a duplicate DT-C139 row in DECISIONS.md. Train 9's rows union kept both the round-1 text and the final
  text of T-703's row; the older one (without the Classic header sentence) is gone, the final one stays.
