T-703 - UX audit Wave 3, widened side panel: a sidebar dragged wide crushes the workspace (DT-C139; UX audit lane C)
Worker 49, coordinator 57, branch s-49/ux-8 from main 80687a7d, 2026-11-03.
Row: proof/ux-audit/requests/W3.md, last section (N2). Follows T-699 / DT-C135, whose known limit this closes.

WHAT WAS WRONG
- T-699 kept the workspace at 340 px or wider only at default sidebar widths.
- The left sidebar drags up to 480 px (VIEW-050). Dragged wide, it kept that width at any window size:
  152 px of workspace at 640 px with the left sidebar alone, 272 px at 1000 px beside the right sidebar.
- The right sidebar is clamped when the app draws it (chrome/RightSidebar.tsx clampWidth), but not again when the
  window shrinks with no re-render: dragged to 900 px at a wide window, then narrowed, the workspace went to 0.

THE RULE (DT-C139)
- The workspace keeps at least 340 CSS px, the T-699 floor, whatever width a sidebar was dragged to.
- Four stylesheet rules in src/styles.css, right after the T-699 step-aside rule:
    .body > .sidebar-wrap:not(.rail-wrap) > .sidebar { width: max(220px, min(var(--sidebar-w), 100vw - 348px)); }
    .body:has(> .rside:not([hidden])) > .sidebar-wrap:not(.rail-wrap) > .sidebar { width: max(220px, min(var(--sidebar-w), 100vw - 588px)); }
    .body > .rside { max-width: max(240px, 100vw - 340px); }
    .body:has(> .sidebar-wrap:not(.rail-wrap)) > .rside { max-width: max(240px, 100vw - 348px - min(var(--sidebar-w), 100vw - 588px)); }
  - 348 = the floor + the left sidebar's 8 px gutter. 588 = that + the right sidebar's 240 px minimum, so a widened left
    sidebar leaves the right one its minimum while it shows.
  - The right sidebar's cap subtracts the left sidebar as drawn, so the two never both take the room.
  - Neither sidebar goes under its own minimum (220 left, 240 right).
  - The Agents screen's rail is also a .sidebar-wrap right under .body; :not(.rail-wrap) leaves it at its own .rail width
    (268 px, Classic 240). The right sidebar only shows on Terminals (shell/window/Right.tsx hidden={!terminals ...}),
    never beside the rail. Review round 1 caught that the first version also widened the rail to --sidebar-w (480 when
    the projects sidebar was dragged wide). Headless Chromium at 1400 px, normal and Classic rail, --sidebar-w 289 and 480:
    main 80687a7d 268/268/240/240, first version 289/480/289/480, this branch 268/268/240/240.
  - A press and release on a narrowed left edge saves the width as drawn (the drag starts there).
- Because the clamps are CSS, they follow the window live: during a drag (the pointer can ask for 480, the sidebar
  stops where the floor is) and when the window shrinks after a drag.
- Order as the window shrinks: the dragged panel gives back its width first; the T-699 step-aside starts only under
  900 px, as before.
- The saved width is what the pointer reached (VIEW-050's 220-480 range is unchanged), so the panel grows back when the
  window widens.
- The drag edge (Grip in chrome/RightSidebar.tsx) now starts a drag from the width as drawn (offsetWidth of the left
  sidebar's wrapper minus its 8 px gutter, or of the right sidebar), not the saved width, so a narrowed panel answers the
  pointer at once instead of after a dead zone.
- The right sidebar's own clamp (clampWidth) is unchanged; the stylesheet cap is never under what it draws (pinned by a
  test), so the rules only catch a width drawn before the window shrank.

KNOWN LIMITS
- With the right sidebar hidden, the left sidebar's 220 px minimum means a window under 568 px can still go below 340.
  Only reachable by zooming far in; the window's own minimum is 900 px at 100 % zoom.
- The right sidebar's tab overflow (More menu) still splits on the width the app computed, not a narrower one the
  stylesheet drew.
