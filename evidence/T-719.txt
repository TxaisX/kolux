T-719 - Under 900 px the right sidebar widens into the room the stepped-aside left sidebar leaves (DT-C155; UX audit lane C)

WHAT WAS WRONG
- Under 900 px the T-699 step-aside hides the left sidebar while the right one shows. Both clamps on the right sidebar still
  subtracted the left sidebar's width:
  - styles.css: `.body:has(> .sidebar-wrap:not(.rail-wrap)) > .rside` max-width (the T-703 rule);
  - chrome/RightSidebar.tsx clampWidth, which Right.tsx uses for drags, arrow keys, the saved width and aria-valuemax
    (sidebarOpen stays true while the stylesheet hides the sidebar).
- Live verify at 800 and 640 px: the right sidebar stayed at 240 px when dragged to the far edge, with the workspace at
  560 and 400 px.

THE FIX (DT-C155)
- styles.css: that rule now sits in `@media (min-width: 900px) { ... }`. Below 900 px `.body > .rside { max-width:
  max(240px, 100vw - 340px) }` applies alone.
- chrome/RightSidebar.tsx clampWidth: `windowWidth - (windowWidth < NARROW_PX ? 0 : leftWidth) - RESERVED`. Below 900 px the
  right sidebar reaches the window less the 400 px reserve (800 px: 400; 640 px: 240), so the workspace keeps >= 400 px.
- From 900 px up nothing changes.
