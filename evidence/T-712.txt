T-712 - Settings menus close and account rows stop nesting buttons (DT-C148; UX audit W3)
Worker 2f, coordinator 57, branch s-2f/settings-menus from main 8c9536aa, 2026-11-03.
Row: proof/ux-audit/requests/W3.md, "T-712 / DT-C148 settings menus" (assigned 14:31). Findings: settings-a.md, destructive.md.

WHAT WAS WRONG
- "Other ways to connect" (OtherWays in settings/ConnectGrid.tsx, also the connected card's Manage menu) was a bare
  <details>: it stayed open after a pick, Escape did nothing to it, and a click elsewhere left it open.
- An account row (settings/AccountRow.tsx) was a role=button div holding Re-authenticate, Name and Remove buttons:
  buttons nested in a button, each inner one needing stopPropagation. The inline Remove confirm ignored Escape and
  its Remove/Keep stayed pressable while the row was busy (a sign-in for that account running).

THE FIX (DT-C148)
- OtherWays: a pick removes the open attribute before it runs; while open, Escape (capture phase, stopped) shuts it
  and puts focus back on the summary, and a pointerdown outside it shuts it. Same behaviour as the chat's HandOff menu.
  No listener exists while the menu is shut, so Escape still closes Settings then.
- AccountRow: the pick is a real <button class="acct-pick"> (same title, aria-pressed, chips) beside the row's own
  buttons; the role=button div, its key handler and the stopPropagation wrappers are gone. The confirm opens with focus
  on Keep; an Escape from inside it calls Keep and is stopped there (a key handler on the confirm, not a document
  listener). While busy, Remove and Keep are disabled and Escape is left to Settings.
- styles.css: the four .acct-row[role="button"] / [data-active] rules now target .acct-pick; the global button reset
  covers font, colour, background and border.
