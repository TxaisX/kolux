Known caveat for a human
  A 206 for a whole-file request is what the brief asked for. For an image or a font over 8 MB that a page loads
  without a Range header, a browser may show only the first chunk (media elements re-request the rest). Files over
  8 MB that are not video, audio or PDF are rare, but if one turns up the answer is to raise CHUNK for non-media or
  to let only media take the chunk path.
