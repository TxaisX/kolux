WHAT WAS WRONG
- The left and right sidebar grips (one shared Grip in chrome/RightSidebar.tsx) were role=separator with no tabIndex, no
  keys and no value: keyboard users could not resize either sidebar, and screen readers announced no width.

THE FIX (DT-C147)
- chrome/RightSidebar.tsx Grip: tabIndex 0, aria-valuenow (the width), aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax (new min/max props).
  Left/Right step 16 px, Shift 64 px, in the direction a drag would (dir: +1 for the left sidebar, -1 for the right).
  Home/End ask for far past either end; the callers' existing clamps land on min/max. Each key press is saved
  (onWidth(w, true)) like the end of a drag. Keys and drags start from the width as drawn (the T-703 helper, now shared).
  Other keys pass through untouched; the four handled keys call preventDefault so nothing behind scrolls.
- shell/window/Sidebar.tsx: the left grip passes LEFT_MIN/LEFT_MAX (220/480).
- shell/window/Right.tsx: one fit() wraps the existing clampWidth; the right sidebar gets max = fit(1e4), i.e. the window
  less the left sidebar and the 400 px reserve; RightSidebar passes RIGHT_MIN/max to its grip.
- styles.css: the grip shows its drag line on :focus-visible as on hover (no outline box on an 8 px strip).
- T-703's 340 px workspace floor is the stylesheet's and is unchanged.
