WHAT CHANGED
- One launch holds at most 24 seats (MAX_SESSIONS); a typed count above that becomes 24, a launch
  asking for more is refused with a message, and growing the lineup past 8 (ASK_ABOVE) asks once
  first. Shrinking never asks.
- A Start that fails part-way says how many new workspaces it already made and refreshes the
  sidebar so they show; spare worktree folders are no longer reused on the next try.
- While Starting, Esc, the sheet's X and Cancel do nothing. Esc in a text box leaves the box first;
  the next Esc closes the page.
- The stored pick is derived from the seat (no stale pick); Install covers seats that share an agent;
  the chooser and the checklist use one canInstall rule.
- The plus menu works from the keyboard (arrows, menu item roles) and does not take focus back
  from a chooser it just opened.
- Notices name their buttons for screen readers; the no-AI-program notice has Open Settings, a
  failed check offers Try again, a dismissal that could not be saved says so.
- spec/02-sessions-launching.md: SESS-013 Rules gain the 24 cap and the ask above 8; its box 2
  acceptance points here; SESS-015 names the cap.

WHY
Static UX audit rows findings/launch-agents.md 2-10 and more: a stray "500" started hundreds of
agents and worktrees, Esc mid-Start left half a launch, and failed Starts left folders unannounced.

REVIEW
Opus review B -> fix pass -> re-review B+ -> plus-menu focus fix -> A-.
