WHAT CHANGED
- Address bar: Ctrl/Cmd+L on a previewed document presses the chip (now a real button, reachable by Tab, Enter and
  Space), so the chord works there too; leaving the chip's text box by Escape puts focus back on the chip.
- Menus: the Import cookies menu, the Preview server menu and the Send to agent menu are real menus. They take focus
  when they open, arrows and Home/End move between rows, Escape closes only the menu and gives focus back to its
  button, and the Send menu also closes on a press outside it.
- Notices: the pane's one-line notice has a Dismiss button and clears itself after 12 s; "No agent sessions in this
  worktree" clears after 5 s. A reload (the button and the chord) says so in the notice when the page will not reload.
- Downloads: a finished, failed or cancelled row stays in the strip until it is dismissed (it used to leave after 8 s),
  and a finished row gains Open. Cancel is not added: it needs a new Rust command.
- Closing: Close tab with a download still running or notes not sent says so, and a second press closes. Clear on the
  notes tray asks "Clear all?" first. Close (and Escape) on the drawing view with marks asks "Discard marks?".
- Drawing: Clear is one Undo step (Undo brings every mark back, Redo clears them again); a text box open on the picture
  is committed where it was opened, also when focus leaves it (Escape still cancels).
- Wording and look: "Copy screenshot", "Send to agent..."; Back, Forward, Reload and Close tab tooltips show the chord
  the keymap really holds (a customised or unbound key is shown as it is); Import cookies uses the folder-plus icon and
  stays visible in a narrow pane (only the Viewport select goes); browser.css uses the defined --line-subtle and
  --surface tokens.
- Not done (listed, not hidden): Allow-folder revoke and the narrow-pane overflow menu (rows 14 and 24, needs Rust or a
  bigger change); the app-level Ctrl+W still closes without the warning (row 25, request filed in requests/C.md).
- spec/13-browser-computer.md: Rules and Acceptance of BROW-003, 005, 006, 013, 019, 028, 035, 037, 038, 039 and 067
  match the above; new Acceptance lines point here.

WHY
Static UX audit of the browser pane found a chip and three menus the keyboard could not reach, notices that never went
away, finished downloads that vanished before anyone saw where the file went, and Clear, Close and tab close that threw
work away on one press. Each fix is the smallest change that gives the keyboard and the second chance back.
