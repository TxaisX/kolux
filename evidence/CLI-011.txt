CLI-011 evidence, 2026-11-01, lane5/CLI-011 (worktree lane-t1, from origin/main a948cc3f)
`kolux open` on a running Kolux now grants the app the foreground before asking it forward, raises its
window from the command's own process the way a second launch does, and reports whether the window
really came to the front (`raised`). Card CLI-011 box 2 (App minimised → kolux open brings it to the
front) stays a live Windows check; this unit makes that check a one-line read of `kolux open --json`.

--- what changed
src-tauri/src/cli/mod.rs:229-266   `open(send, launch, grant, raise, limit)`: `grant()` runs before the first ask;
    the launch-and-retry road is unchanged; after the answer the pid in `result.pid` goes to `raise(pid)` and its
    verdict is put into the result as `raised` (true / false / null when no pid was named or the platform cannot tell).
src-tauri/src/cli/mod.rs:680-689   the client: `grant` reads the app's pid from the startup file (lifecycle::read_running,
    cli.json) and calls lifecycle::allow_foreground(pid) (AllowSetForegroundWindow); `raise` calls
    lifecycle::raise_by_pid(pid, RAISE_TRIES, sleep) on Windows (restore if iconic, SetForegroundWindow, checked with
    GetForegroundWindow, 5 tries 150 ms apart) and answers None elsewhere. Both lifecycle helpers were already pub.
src-tauri/src/cli/text.rs:368-371  "Kolux is ready; its window is in front" only when `raised` is not false; when it is
    false: "Kolux is ready; its window was restored, but Windows kept another window in front". A started app keeps
    "Kolux started and is ready".
src-tauri/src/cli/mod.rs:1041-1049 (`app.open` on the app's side) is unchanged: it still unminimizes, shows and focuses
    through ctx.front and fails the request when the window is gone; the new dispatch test pins that.
docs/guide/command-line.md         the `kolux open` row and a paragraph on the two lines and `result.raised`.
docs/live-proofs-windows.md B-5    now also asks for `kolux open --json` printing "raised": true.
DECISIONS.md DT-C69                the result shape {ready, started, pid, raised} and the two readable lines.
