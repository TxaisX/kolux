====================================================================================================================

Scope: the chip and data-key parts of T-566. The Tip card part belongs to the product-ux lane (T-612) and was not touched.

WHY THE CHIP WAS MISSING ON RELAUNCH 2, PANE 1 (T-544 run 4)
------------------------------------------------------------
The chip was not skipped by the pane. It was shown and then cleared by Escape key presses from the prover itself:

- evidence/T-544.txt lines 44-53: on relaunch 2 (and only there) a Tip card was open, and the prover logged
  "closed a first-run dialog: Tip (Close); still open: true" ten times.
- scripts/prove/live-common.mjs:252-262 dismissFirstRun: for a dialog that is not "Skip setup?" and has no
  "Close setup" button, it presses Escape (line 260: app.cdp("key", "Escape")). The log line says "(Close)" for
  both the button click and the Escape. The Tip card has no "Close setup" button (only Wizard.tsx:61 has one), so
  all ten were Escape presses, not clicks on a Close button.
- src/onboarding/Guides.tsx:420: the Tip card is non-modal (aria-modal="false") and closes on Escape only through
  its own onKeyDown, i.e. only when the keyboard is inside the card. ONB-027 (src/onboarding/tipView.test.ts) keeps
  typing focus in the restored pane on purpose. So each Escape went to the focused terminal pane.
- Pane 1 was the focused pane in every round (the prover types into pane 1 last: live-T-544.mjs:426 and :445).
- In the pane, Escape has no Kolux binding, routeKey gives "shell", the custom key handler returns true
  (TerminalPane.tsx key handler; kittyKeys.ts latinControl returns null for a bare Escape), xterm handles the key and
  fires term.onKey, and TERM-107's rule "disappears on the first key press or click in the pane" clears the chip.
- Pane 0 (not focused) kept its chip on relaunch 2; rounds 1 and 3 had no Tip card, so no key was sent and both
  panes kept their chips. live-T-544.mjs back() (lines 290-299) reads the chips after dismissFirstRun, so its own
  keys reach the pane before the read, although its comment (line 279) says the read comes before any click.

So whether the chip survived depended on whether a Tip card was open at that relaunch, which decides whether the
prover sends keys before reading the chips. That is the timing that skipped it.

The one real timing gap in the pane itself: the chip was set only inside the restore, after the pane's first IPC
step (four event-listener registrations awaited together, TerminalPane.tsx "ux card 6"), and its key/click
listener was registered at the same point. A pane read in that gap had no chip, and a key pressed in that gap was
not counted. That gap is what this change closes.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
- src/terminal/TerminalPane.tsx:324-327: the mark is decided from the props (restored, resumed) at the first render
  (useState initial value), so a restored pane carries "restored" or "restored without resume" from its first paint.
- src/terminal/TerminalPane.tsx:469-477: the clearing listeners (term.onKey and mousedown on the pane box) are
  registered synchronously at mount, right after term.open, before any await. The old copies inside the async restore
  (old lines 997-1004) are deleted.
- src/terminal/TerminalPane.tsx:1233: the effect cleanup removes the pane box's mousedown listener.
- src/terminal/TerminalPane.tsx:1490: the pane's <section> carries data-key={number} (number is the saved pane key,
  App.tsx passes number={p.key}); a pane without a key renders no attribute.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
- scripts/prove/pane-states.mjs (new): exports paneStates(document, sel), the reader that used to be an inline string in
  live-T-544.mjs. key = the pane's own data-key (TerminalPane.tsx data-key={number}, the saved layout key); a pane
  without one falls back to its sidebar row (ProjectsPanel .sess.pane-row[data-pane], matched by title "Show <title>");
  restored = the text of .status.restored or null; hidden panes (offsetParent null) are skipped. Self-contained: the
  prover sends it to the page as text, (${readPaneStates.toString()})(document, TERM), the way it sends domRows.
- scripts/prove/live-T-544.mjs back(): waits for the pane and its input field, sleeps 4 s, reads paneStates() ONCE into a
  local, THEN dismissTip() / dismissFirstRun() / dismissTip(), logs that first snapshot on the "reattach <label>" line as
  before, and logs a second read on "reattach <label>: panes after dialogs ..." so the Escape effect is visible in the
  evidence. Before this change dismissTip() and dismissFirstRun() ran first (old lines 291-292), so dismissFirstRun's
  Escape (live-common.mjs:260, sent when a dialog has no "Close setup" button; the Tip card has none and is non-modal)
  reached the focused pane and cleared its chip before the read: the exact cause of "no chip on relaunch 2, pane 1".
